The return

A projected 9.4% p.a. — based on a real operating model.

The 9.4% figure is a projection. It is not a guarantee, and it is not a promise of what any single year will pay.

It comes out of a production model built for this site: how much electricity the plant is expected to generate, the assumptions behind that figure, and commissioning in Q3 2027. If those assumptions turn out differently, the return turns out differently.

Water

What happens in a dry year?

A run-of-river plant generates from the water flowing past it. Some years carry more water than others. Production varies with it.

The production model for this site is built on 22 years of measured river-flow data. That does not remove the variation, and we would not claim it does. What it does is show where the forecast comes from.

The full flow series and the assumptions applied to it are in the Investor Pack, so you can check the model yourself rather than take a number on trust.

The point isn’t to pretend every year will look the same. It’s to show what the forecast is based on.
