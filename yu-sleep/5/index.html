By Abigail M. | Health & Wellness

After menopause, everything changed…

Falling asleep was torture.

Racing through every worry, every regret, everything I forgot to do.

And then — even when I finally DID fall asleep — I'd jolt awake at 2… 3… 4 AM.

Heart already pounding.

And I'd lie there again.

Watching the clock…

Calculating how many hours I had left…

Knowing the next day was going to be another zombie day.

After a few google searches at 4 AM…

I was convinced I had insomnia…

So I did what everyone does…

I started taking melatonin.

3mg at first… Helped a little.

Then it stopped working.

So I went to 5mg.

Then 10mg…

And here's what nobody tells you about melatonin…

The more you take, the more you need.

And the MORE you take?

The worse the next morning feels.

I was waking up so groggy I could barely form a sentence before 10am.

Foggy. Heavy. Like someone had put wet cement in my brain overnight.

But I was still waking up at 3am.

So the melatonin was making my mornings worse… without fixing my nights.

And when I tried to stop taking it?

My sleep completely fell apart.

My body had forgotten how to sleep without it.

I was trapped.

And the worst part…

By 8pm every evening I'd feel this knot forming in my stomach. Because I already knew what was coming…

Another night of lying there… Another ceiling… Alone with my thoughts while everyone else slept.

I'd been dealing with this for almost three years when a friend mentioned something called "the 30-second cherry trick".

I almost rolled my eyes.

But I was desperate enough to try one more thing.

So that night — 30 minutes before bed — I tried it.

And I don't know how to explain what happened next…

The racing thoughts just… quieted.
